## Mini Poseidon

Mini Poseidon is a NetLogo fisheries agent-based model originally developed
by Nicolas Payette.

**Original author:** Nicolas Payette  
**Copyright:** © 2023 Nicolas Payette  
**License:** MIT License

Original repository:  
https://github.com/nicolaspayette/mini-poseidon

The original repository describes Mini Poseidon as a small NetLogo fisheries
ABM inspired by the POSEIDON model.

The MIT License permits use, copying, modification and redistribution,
provided that the original copyright notice and license notice are preserved.

The full license for the original Mini Poseidon implementation is available at:

https://github.com/nicolaspayette/mini-poseidon/blob/main/LICENSE

Related POSEIDON publication:

https://doi.org/10.1007/s11625-018-0579-9
